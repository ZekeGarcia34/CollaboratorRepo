Subtracting integers means finding the difference between two whole numbers, including positive and negative numbers.

The most important rule is:

Keep the first number, change subtraction to addition, and change the sign of the second number.

Rule
$$ a-b = a+(-b) $$

In other words:

Keep → Change → Change

Keep the first number.
Change − to +.
Change the sign of the second number.
Example 1: Positive − Positive
$$ 7-3 $$

Keep 7, change subtraction to addition, and change +3 to −3:

$$ 7+(-3)=4 $$

Answer: 4
